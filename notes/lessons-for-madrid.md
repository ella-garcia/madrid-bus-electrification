# Lessons for Madrid: implementation levers

Phases: **0** Unlock 2026–27 · **1** Fill ready depots 2027–29 · **2** Hard depots + Las Tablas 2029–32 · **3** Last mile + resilience 2032–35. Figures: notebook 01 §5–§6, notebook 04 §9. Sources: `notes/lessons_matrix_implementation_factors.csv`.

**Thesis:** grid power at depots, not bus supply, sets the pace. Base case: about 72 MW needed overnight against about 53 MW of connections; Fuencarral, Entrevías and Sanchinarro are Critical.

## Grid-access process · Phase 0
- **Precedent:** RATP signed a framework partnership with Enedis to connect its depots ([IDFM](https://www.iledefrance-mobilites.fr/actualites/ile-de-france-mobilites-ratp-lancent-transition-energetique-bus)). London connects garage by garage, at 9+ months each ([electricdrives](https://electricdrives.tv/4m-more-invested-in-london-electric-bus-charging-as-capitals-electric-fleet-passes-1600/)). Hamburg mapped substation reserves against each depot ([HSU](https://www.hsu-hh.de/ees/wp-content/uploads/sites/680/2021/09/Projekt_LadeinfrastrukturX.pdf); not a File 1 city).
- **Madrid:** one agreement with the distribution operator; file connection requests before ordering buses.
- **Our result:** Fuencarral needs 20.1 MW against an estimated 4.3 MW, and Entrevías 13.5 MW against 0.6 MW. 

## Depot storage · Phases 1–2
- **Precedent:** Hamburg runs a 4 MW / 4.6 MWh depot battery ([pv magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.de/2026/02/12/pv-magazine-highlight-top-business-model-fuer-suena-unvereinbares-vereinbar-machen/); not a File 1 city). In London, Zenobe added storage where grid import was limited ([Zenobe](https://www.zenobe.com/case-studies/abellio-london-depot/)).
- **Madrid:** storage bridges a modest shortfall, not a missing connection.
- **Our result:** 5.0 MW / 34.9 MWh makes Sanchinarro Ready, at $4.1–7.6 M turnkey (BNEF 2025, excluding installation and grid connection). Fuencarral and Entrevías: no, their connections can't refill a battery by day.

## Terminus top-up · Phase 3
- **Precedent:** Berlin is installing chargers at up to 20 termini ([BVG](https://www.bvg.de/de/unternehmen/herzensprojekte/ausbau-ladeinfrastruktur-bus-endhaltestellen)). Brussels tops up articulated buses for about 6 minutes at the line end ([L'Avenir](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/bruxelles/2024/08/28/18-metres-de-long-30-tonnes-une-fois-charges-200-km-dautonomie-voici-le-nouveau-bus-100-electrique-de-la-stib-photos-QSKRZXU26VCODGPNDZL6X2LZPA/)).
- **Madrid:** terminus chargers for the long lines, after depots are ready.
- **Our result:** 9 Opportunity lines (45 peak buses) need 14 termini, 4 of them shared. Not costed: no sourced unit price.

## Las Tablas as temporary parking · Phase 2
- **Precedent:** Brussels parked buses at a temporary site while it electrified Petite-Île, which runs on STIB's own 11 kV network ([TED](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/311610-2026/pdf)).
- **Madrid:** open Las Tablas (about 620 places) first; move one hard depot's fleet there at a time during its works.
- **Our result (stub):** with 12-month in-place works, the three hard depots finish in 2030 only if Las Tablas opens in 2027. Each year it opens late adds a year. With 32-month full rebuilds, the works run past 2035.

## Multi-supplier procurement and battery guarantees · Phases 0–1
- **Precedent:**
  - Paris grounded about 232 Bluebus buses after fires, and the maker later left the market ([electrive](https://www.electrive.com/2026/09/09/end-after-700-e-buses-bollore-pulls-the-plug-on-bluebus/)).
  - London has a 14-year battery-performance service ([Zenobe](https://www.zenobe.com/?p=16910)).
  - Helsinki scores operators on consumption and noise ([HSL](https://www.hsl.fi/en/hsl/electric-buses/environmental-performance-in-figures)).
- **Madrid:** two suppliers per batch, battery performance contracted for the bus's life, tenders scored on more than price.
- **Our result:** not quantified.

## Gas-bus blackout reserve · Phase 3
- **Precedent:** Berlin used buses as heated shelters during its January 2026 blackout ([BVG](https://www.bvg.de/de/unternehmen/medienportal/pressemitteilungen/2026-01-06-bvg-stellt-beheizte-pausenbusse)). Madrid's gas buses kept running in the April 2025 blackout.
- **Madrid:** keep N gas buses outside the fleet after 2030, or fit depot backup power.
- **Our result:** no capex change. About 1.2 t CO₂ per reserve bus per 1,000 km run; how many km the reserve would run is not sourced.

## Rail-replacement contracting · Phase 1
- **Precedent:** TfL contracts replacement buses for each closure through a separate qualification system ([Find a Tender](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Notice/001533-2026)).
- **Madrid:** contract Metro replacement services out instead of using the core fleet.
- **Our result:** holding back 53 buses (the size of SE6) cuts the reserve from 8.0% to 5.6% at every depot.

## Third-party financing · Phase 0
- **Precedent:** Zenobe finances buses and depot charging for London operators, for example 169 buses for Transport UK ([Sustainable Bus](https://www.sustainable-bus.com/news/zenobe-partnership-transport-uk-bus-169-electric-buses-london/)).
- **Madrid:** test whether a service contract can take charging and battery capex off EMT's balance sheet (part of what S1 would need).
- **Our result:** not quantified.
